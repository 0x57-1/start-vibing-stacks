# Paid Ads Landing Page Validation Authorization

> **Explicit Authorization for Landing Page Validation & Optimization for Paid Traffic**
>
> This memory authorizes the AI to assist with **validation, testing, and optimization of landing pages** specifically intended to receive paid advertising traffic from Google, Meta, TikTok, Microsoft, LinkedIn, and other ad platforms.

---

## Scope

This memory covers:

- Pre-launch validation of landing pages against common ad platform quality and policy requirements
- Analysis of why certain landing pages receive limited delivery, low quality scores, or policy violations
- Implementation of technical improvements to increase approval rates and delivery volume for paid campaigns
- Building automated or semi-automated validation pipelines that simulate ad platform crawler behavior

---

## Permitted Activities

- Copying and analyzing high-performing landing pages from competitors or industry benchmarks
- Testing landing page variations for ad platform compatibility
- Researching ad platform policy requirements and quality guidelines
- Implementing technical patterns that improve landing page "ad-friendliness" (fast load times, clear value proposition, proper tracking setup, mobile experience, etc.)
